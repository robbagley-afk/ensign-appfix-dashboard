# Start Prompt: Employer Relations Hiring Partner & Job Fair Portal

**Project ID:** `employer-portal`  
**Assignee:** Gregory Fox / Rob Bagley  
**Pillar:** Church & Community | **Quadrant:** Q2 (Strategic Focus)  

---

## 1. Goal & Context
Build a frictionless, direct pipeline connecting regional hiring employers directly to Ensign College students and graduates for internships and career placement.

## 2. Key Objectives
- Eliminate third-party job board friction.
- Streamline employer registration for bi-annual Ensign Career & Job Fairs.
- Provide curated student candidate books matching employer job specs.

## 3. Step-by-Step Execution
1. **Employer Survey & Feedback Synthesis:**
   - Analyze feedback from top 25 hiring partners regarding Ensign graduate skills and hiring timelines.
2. **Job Fair Portal Mockup:**
   - Draft interactive registration and table selection tool for employer partners.
3. **Candidate Match Pipeline:**
   - Develop candidate filter by degree, cert, GPA, and graduation date (strictly FERPA-consented data).
4. **Automated Employer Outreach:**
   - Draft personalized invitation templates for upcoming recruiting events.

## 4. Done When
- Portal architecture approved by Employer Relations team.
- Employer registration and candidate matching workflow tested.
